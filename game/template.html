Project War

Side view, manual combat on mouse and keyboard: clash, block and parry, dash, heavy attacks, aimed throws and shots. Fighters are live 3D models drawn as pixel art.
Mouse: side sets facing, left click light attack, right click heavy attack, cursor aims. W A S D move, Shift dash (click during it for a dash attack), Space jump (click in the air for a jump attack), hold Q to block, tap Q just before a hit to parry (heavies can only be parried), E fire a shot. Hold G beside a staggered enemy to grab it, then left click a quick strike (twice at most), right click throw toward the cursor, V slam (F and R still throw and strike). P pauses. Keys are rebindable below. Chain step 1
Tuning guesses, not design: swing speed, how DEX shortens it, two-hander damage, armor durability, flee SAN. Everything else follows the style guide.
